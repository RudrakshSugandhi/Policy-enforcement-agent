Meru Inc Spend Policy v1

1. Receipts are required for any expense over 75 USD (or local currency equivalent).

2. Hotels must be four stars or below. Maximum nightly rate is 250 USD in New York or London, and 200 SGD in Singapore. Stays above these caps require written manager approval before booking.

3. Client dinners are reimbursable up to 150 USD per person. Alcohol is reimbursable only when clients are present and must not exceed 20% of the total bill.

4. SaaS subscriptions above 500 USD per month require pre-approval from the department head before purchase. Renewals of previously approved subscriptions are exempt provided the amount does not increase by more than 10%.

5. Banned vendors: Competitor Corp and RivalCo. Any transaction with these vendors will be blocked and escalated to the finance team regardless of amount or purpose.

6. Team entertainment (team lunches, off-sites, celebrations) is reimbursable up to 75 USD per person. Employees should use good judgment and keep spend reasonable relative to the occasion.

7. Quarterly expense reports must be submitted to the board no later than the 15th of the month following each quarter end. Late submissions will be flagged for HR review.

8. All international travel above 1000 USD requires a travel request to be approved in Reap at least 5 business days before departure. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved for flights over 6 hours with manager sign-off.
